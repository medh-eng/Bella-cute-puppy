Privacy Policy — Bella the Cute Puppy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Bella the Cute Puppy is published by Medhaneeka Healthcare Private Limited, Nagpur, India ("we").

Bella the Cute Puppy is a game for young children. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information — from children or from anyone else.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. It does not use the internet and does not ask for any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or advertising ID).

Information saved on your device

The game stores Bella’s progress, including her needs, treats, outfits, learned tricks, stickers, days played and sound settings, in a local game-save file. It also writes limited technical diagnostics (such as missing game assets and the game/Unity version) locally to help identify setup problems. The app does not send these files to us or to an analytics or advertising provider. These files are not designed to contain personal information.

Parents can delete saved game progress using the “Start over” control in the grown-ups’ area (gear icon). Uninstalling the app normally deletes its local files. Depending on the device’s operating-system backup settings, a device backup may preserve or restore app files. The “Start over” control does not separately delete the local diagnostics file; uninstalling normally removes it.

Children's privacy

Bella is designed for young children. The app does not ask children to provide a name, contact details, location, photo or other personal information, and it does not use advertising, behavioural tracking or social features. If you have a concern about a child’s privacy, contact us using the email below.

Third parties

The current game has no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google Play and the Android operating system operate independently of Bella and may process data in accordance with their own privacy terms. Visiting this website is separate from playing the offline game; the hosting provider may process basic web request logs needed to serve this page.

Data security and retention

We do not operate a game-account database or a game-data server for Bella, so we do not retain copies of Bella’s game progress on our systems. The locally saved files remain subject to your device’s storage, backup and access controls. The game does not transmit player data across the internet.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.
