Privacy Policy — Bella the Cute Puppy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Bella the Cute Puppy is a game for young children. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information — from children or from anyone else.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. It does not use the internet and does not ask for any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or advertising ID).

What the game saves on the device

Only the puppy's own progress: her needs, treats, outfits, learned tricks, stickers, the number of days played and the sound settings. This file stays on the device. It is never sent anywhere, and it contains no name, age, photo, device identifier or anything else that could identify a person.

The saved progress is deleted when the game is uninstalled, or at any time from the grown-ups' area inside the game (gear icon → "Start over").

Children's privacy

The game is designed for children and is offered under the Google Play Families Policy. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties and nothing for which parental consent is required under laws such as COPPA (United States), the GDPR (European Union) or the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India).

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising or analytics software and no links that leave the game. It is distributed through Google Play; Google's own handling of store downloads is described in Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.
