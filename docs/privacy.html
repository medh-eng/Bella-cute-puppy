Privacy Policy — Medhaneeka Games website

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy covers the website games.medhaneekahealthcare.in, run by Medhaneeka Healthcare Private Limited, Nagpur, India ("we"). Each of our games has its own privacy policy, listed at the end of this page.

What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you. The site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts. Every page is a plain file with no code loaded from other companies.

Server logs

Like almost every website, the servers of our hosting provider automatically record basic technical details of each visit, such as the IP address, the time, the page requested and the browser type. These logs are used to keep the service secure and working. We do not use them to identify visitors or to build profiles.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not pass it to anyone else.

Children

This website does not ask children, or anyone else, for personal information.

Links to other sites

This site links to our company website and, once a game is released, to its page on Google Play. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this website ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
