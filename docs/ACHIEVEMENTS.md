# What Bella the Cute Puppy is, and what's actually built

A running log of what this project has become, for Pon to look back on. Technical status and
next-steps live in `docs/NEXT_CHAT.md` — this page is the "what we achieved" view.

## The idea

A free, ad-free, offline virtual-pet mobile game (Boo-style) for Android/Google Play. One puppy,
Bella, that players care for, dress up, play with and grow attached to. No real-money purchases,
no networking, no third-party art or audio licences — every asset (model, animation, icon, sound,
music) is either AI-generated by Pon (Meshy/Tripo3D, for Bella's own body) or built from scratch in
code (everything else — see "How content is made" below).

## Milestone: Unity day (16 Nov 2026)

For months this was all built blind — scripts compiling against hand-written stand-ins for the
Unity API, animation checked in Blender, never once run inside the real engine. On 16 Nov 2026 Pon
installed Unity 6.3 LTS with Android Build Support and opened the project for the first time, and
**it worked**: Bella stood in her room, walked, ate, napped, fetched, took a bath, learned tricks,
and wore outfits, first try.

## What's in the game right now

**The core loop** — five needs (hunger, hygiene, energy, health, happiness) that decay over real
time, including while the app is closed (with a floor so leaving her alone never feels punishing).
Petting, feeding, bathing, sleeping and playing restore them. A day/night cycle changes the light,
the music and the ambience (birdsong by day, crickets at night).

**Six mini-games**, each its own shop/activity screen:
- **Feeding** — buy kibble (free), a treat meal, or a fancy meal with Treats; each has its own
  hunger and happiness effect (added 16 Nov 2026, replacing a single plain "feed" tap).
- **Bath** — a bubble-wash game.
- **Tricks** — draw a gesture (line, circle, zigzag...) to teach Bella sit, beg, paw, spin, roll
  over, dance.
- **Wardrobe** — buy and wear hats, bows, glasses, bandanas, capes and crowns; 19 outfit pieces
  across 4 shapes-plus-colour-variants as of 16 Nov 2026.
- **Clinic** — a gentle "pick the tool" check-up game, no fail state.
- **Sticker book** — 19 stickers for milestones (first bath, a week together, fashion sense, a
  garden of butterflies...).

**The room** has day/night lighting, drifting clouds and stars, tappable butterflies by day,
fireflies and a nightlight by night, bunting, a toy shelf and a toy basket.

**Sound**: 5 original music loops (one per time of day, one for mini-games), 2 looping nature
ambience beds, and ~25 voice/effect clips — including, as of 16 Nov 2026, four new puppy moods
(an extra-happy bark, a sleepy yawn, a sad whine, a hungry whimper) that play automatically when
she's genuinely happy, tired, neglected or hungry.

## How content is made (and why it's free)

Only Bella's own rigged 3D body came from an AI 3D-model tool (Meshy/Tripo3D). Everything else —
clothes, food, icons, sound effects, music — is generated by small Python/code pipelines that ship
in the repo (`tools/pipeline/gen_icons.py`, `tools/pipeline/gen_audio.py`, plus in-engine procedural
shapes for clothing in `Accessories.cs`). That means new clothing colours, new food items and new
icons cost nothing and need no AI credits — they're a few lines of code. The one real limit: the
synthesised voice/mood sounds are built from scratch out of sine waves and noise, so they read as
an expressive cartoon dog, not a recorded one — genuine realism there would need either a licensed
sample library or a paid AI audio model, both outside the zero-spend plan.

## Known, fixed bugs worth remembering

- **Wardrobe camera bug (16 Nov 2026)**: the dress-up screen framed the camera at ground level
  instead of Bella's torso, so her head — and anything worn on it — sat above the visible frame.
  Fixed in `WardrobeActivity.cs` (look-height `0.02` → `0.6`, matching Bath/Clinic/Tricks).
- Floor-clipping animations, a holder-object normalising bug, tight treat economy, UI clipping on
  tall/wide phones, and opaque popup portraits — all found and fixed without Unity, see
  `docs/NEXT_CHAT.md` §1 for the full table.

## Not done yet

- No Play Console listing, closed test, or public release yet.
- Feeding-shop treat prices are a first guess and may need tuning once played for real.
