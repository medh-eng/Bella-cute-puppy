Character limits are Google's; the counts were checked when this was written.

```
Bella the Cute Puppy: Pet Care
```

> Do **not** put "free", "no ads", "best", "#1" or emoji in the app name, icon or developer name —

## Full description (max 4000)

```
Meet Bella — a tiny, very happy golden puppy who can't wait to see you.

Bella the Cute Puppy is a calm, kind virtual-pet game made for young children. There is nothing to read, nothing to buy and nothing to lose: just a puppy who loves being looked after.

LOOK AFTER BELLA
• Feed her when her thought bubble shows a bone
• Give her a warm bubble bath — scrub, rinse and dry
• Tuck her into bed when she is sleepy
• Throw her ball and watch her run
• Stroke her and watch her tail wag faster

TEACH HER TRICKS
Draw a shape on the screen with your finger and Bella answers with a trick. A line down means sit. A circle means roll over. A zigzag means dance! Teach her all six.

DRESS HER UP
Earn treats by caring for Bella and choose bows, hats, glasses, bandanas, capes and a golden crown from her wardrobe.

VISIT THE CLINIC
Sometimes Bella gets the sniffles. Pick the right tool and she is bouncing again in no time. Gentle, never scary.

COME BACK TOMORROW
• A present every day
• A sticker book that fills up with happy moments
• Bella slowly grows from a little puppy into a big puppy
• Her room follows the real time of day — sunny mornings, sleepy nights

MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Big picture buttons — no reading needed
• No way to fail. Bella is always happy you tried
• Bella is never hurt, sad for long, or upset with you for being away

MADE FOR PARENTS' PEACE OF MIND
• No adverts
• No in-app purchases
• No accounts, no chat, no links out of the game
• Works completely offline and collects no data at all
• Settings are behind a grown-ups-only gate

Bella is waiting. Come and say hello!
```
